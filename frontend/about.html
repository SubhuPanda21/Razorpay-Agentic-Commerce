ABOUT

Why this exists.

Give an autonomous agent a payment tool and a prompt, and it decides how much to spend. That works — until the one time it doesn't. This project is an answer: an AI checkout where the spending limit is enforced by code, not by asking nicely.

Built for Razorpay's AI Buildathon

Track 01 — AI Growth & Agentic Commerce is the primary build. Rather than four separate demos for four tracks, this is one coherent pipeline where Risk, Recovery, and Finance are composed stages the same order flows through — a shopping agent selects a product, a policy engine enforces hard spending caps no agent can override, a risk gate screens for fraud, payment recovers itself with bounded retries, and a finance agent reconciles the result. Every step lands in one audit trail.
